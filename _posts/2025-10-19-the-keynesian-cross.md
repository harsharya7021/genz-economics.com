## TL;DR

- **Planned expenditure** `E = C(Y−T) + I + G`; equilibrium when `Y = E`.
- The 45° line is where actual output equals what people plan to spend.
- The **multiplier** shows why a small change in autonomous spending has an outsized effect on output.

## What Session 2 covered

- Shift from the long-run framing to the short run, where prices are sticky.
- The consumption function `C(Y−T)` and the marginal propensity to consume (MPC).
- The 45° diagram; solving for equilibrium `Y*`.
- The expenditure multiplier `1 / (1 − MPC)` and why its real-world value is usually well below textbook numbers.
