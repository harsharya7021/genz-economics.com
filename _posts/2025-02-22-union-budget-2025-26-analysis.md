## TL;DR

- What the **headline fiscal deficit** actually signals about the fiscal stance.
- The tilt from revenue expenditure to **capital expenditure** — and whether the multiplier story holds.
- Changes in the personal tax structure and what they imply for disposable income and consumption.

## What this session covered

A standalone discussion separate from the lecture sequence — the class read the Union Budget 2025-26 through the macro lens we'd built so far. Topics:

- Fiscal deficit trajectory and FRBM glide path.
- Composition of spending: capex push vs. subsidies.
- Direct and indirect tax changes.
- What the Budget implies for monetary-fiscal coordination over the next year.
