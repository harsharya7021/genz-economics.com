## TL;DR

- **Real exchange rate** `q = e · P*/P`: nominal rate adjusted for price levels.
- **Balassa-Samuelson**: countries with higher productivity in tradables have higher price levels in non-tradables → stronger real exchange rates.
- That's why a chai in Mumbai and a coffee in Zurich both "cost locally reasonable amounts" but convert to very different dollar prices.

## What Session 5 covered
