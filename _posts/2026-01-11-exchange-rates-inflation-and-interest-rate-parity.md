## TL;DR

- **CIP** (covered interest parity): forward premium = interest rate differential. Holds tightly in free markets.
- **UIP** (uncovered interest parity): expected depreciation = interest rate differential. Holds loosely at best.
- Inflation differentials pass through to nominal exchange rates in the long run (relative PPP).

## What Session 6 covered

- CIP derivation and empirical fit.
- UIP, the "forward premium puzzle", and carry trade.
- How RBI thinks about the rupee given US rates and domestic inflation.
- What this means for a Gen Z investor looking at USD returns vs. INR returns.
