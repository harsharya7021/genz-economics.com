## TL;DR

- The **MP curve** replaces LM: the central bank sets the real interest rate in response to inflation and output.
- Taylor-rule intuition: when inflation is above target, raise; when output is below potential, cut.
- IS-MP is closer to how actual inflation-targeting regimes (including the RBI since 2016) operate.

## What Session 3A covered

- Problems with LM — money supply isn't what modern central banks target.
- Introducing the MP curve and the policy reaction function.
- Shifting the MP curve: demand shocks vs. supply shocks.
