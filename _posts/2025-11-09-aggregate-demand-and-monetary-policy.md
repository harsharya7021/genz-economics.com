## TL;DR

- Aggregate demand slopes down because lower price levels → higher real money → lower rates → more investment and consumption.
- The central bank influences demand by setting the short-term rate.
- The transmission mechanism has several steps — don't confuse "RBI cuts rate" with "demand goes up" in a straight line.

## What Session 3 covered

- Derivation of the AD curve from the earlier Keynesian model.
- How interest rates affect `I` (investment) and durable consumption.
- Intro to the RBI's policy rate and the operating framework.
