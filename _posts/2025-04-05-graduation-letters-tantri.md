*From **The Tantri Files** — verbatim writing from Prof. Tantri's WhatsApp messages, with his blessing. Two short notes — one to Hyderabad on April 5, 2025, one to Mohali the next day — sent on the day each batch was wrapping up.*

---

## To the Hyderabad batch — April 5, 2025

> Dear Hyderabad folks,
>
> Whatever you do from hereon and wherever you go from here, please **reserve 1 hour a day for learning something new.**
>
> It could be about anything, from music to science.
>
> Trust me, this will have a transformative impact.
>
> **Let the learning begin.**

---

## To the Mohali batch — April 6, 2025

> Dear Mohali,
>
> Unfortunately, I will not be able to come tomorrow. Too much travel has caught up and impacted my health a bit.
>
> As you go back to the real world, please start thinking like future leaders.
>
> Please **treat any job you do as an art and you an artist.**
>
> Unlike what they teach in MBA, I believe that more than the customer you should be happy with your work and enjoy it.
>
> All the best. I will be in touch with you for a long time. All the best.

---

## And, threaded through that same week

A few of the smaller messages from the same week, clipped without his even meaning them as advice:

> **Tantri.** Life will be so good when all of us start seeing ourselves as artists, including the hard-nosed investment banker and the lawyer. The sense of fulfilment is beyond words in such a world. The best part is, in most such cases, money also follows. Sadly, most of us are absorbed by mundane considerations.

> **Tantri.** I am realising how difficult it is to sit and listen. **Faculty who don't give breaks should be prosecuted.**

> **Tantri.** Faculty not stepping out of campus is a disaster for education.

---
