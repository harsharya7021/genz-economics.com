## TL;DR

- **IS curve** = goods-market equilibrium (`Y = C + I + G`), downward-sloping in `(Y, r)`.
- **LM curve** = money-market equilibrium, upward-sloping in `(Y, r)`.
- Fiscal expansion shifts IS right: output and rates both rise; part of the stimulus is offset by **crowding out** of private investment.

## What Session 4 covered

- Building the IS curve from the goods-market block.
- Building the LM curve from money demand `L(Y, r)`.
- Comparative statics: what `ΔG`, `ΔT`, and `ΔM` each do.
- The crowding-out debate in the Indian context — why the fiscal deficit matters.
