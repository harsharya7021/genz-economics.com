The timetable · public — bring your brother-in-law

The system runs on a schedule.

CPI lands on the 12th. WPI on the 14th. IIP on the 28th. The MPC meets six times a year on dates the RBI publishes every March, and money-supply data drops every Friday at five. None of this is secret — it is just never in one place. Here it is in one place, with what to watch in each print. The whole point of the course is that when the number drops, you already know what question to ask it.
