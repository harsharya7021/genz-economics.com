## What this is

Prof. Prasanna Tantri's macro, in one room. The notes from each session, the
lectures, and the papers and circulars worth your time — collected, so they stop
living in one student's folder and start compounding.

It is not a textbook and it is not trying to be. It is a *living* set of notes,
calibrated for people who want to understand what is actually happening in the
economy. Not storytelling. Useful knowledge.

## Why it's closed

We tried it in the open. A handful of people did the work; everyone else watched.
And the material here — papers, RBI circulars, draft notes — is calibrated for
people who have the background to use it correctly. Without that context, the same
document misleads more than it helps.

So, on a serious note: the login is not a fee. It is a gate — so the people reading
this are the people it was written for. This is not about exclusivity. It is about
not wasting your time, or ours.

## Why you sign in

So the useful stuff stays with the people who will use it. Sign in once, and it
stays open for you. You also get one note a week — the kind that lets you say
something authoritative the next time someone talks nonsense about the rupee.

## Why one lecture a week

Because you forget. One session a week is not a delivery schedule — it is a recall
device. You see the idea, you sit with it, and you get the next one before the last
one fades. That is how it sticks.

## Who's behind it

Built on Prof. Prasanna Tantri's ISB sessions, put together by Harsh. Think of this
as a 25-year plan, not a 12-month one — it will keep expanding. Your job is to keep
reading, and to push back when the data says something different from what is
written here.
