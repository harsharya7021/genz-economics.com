The Wage Watch

The missing variable.

₹41 lakh crore of new money either becomes GDP or becomes prices — and the professor's deciding variable is wage pressure at the lower end, which no agency measures in time to matter. You, however, are in the real world: you hire, you set salaries, you negotiate with vendors. Report what you see. Two minutes. Anonymous — no name, no email, no account id stored, ever.

Nothing identifying is stored — not your name, email, sign-in or account id. One report per month per browser is the honour system.

Filed. Useful knowledge.

The kind no terminal sells — it exists only because people on the ground write it down. The aggregate goes to class once the sample is worth discussing, and feeds the Money Printer. Come back next month; this is a series, not a poll.

Why this exists. Session 19's fork: if the past year's money-supply surge meets idle capacity, it becomes output; if it meets wage pressure, it becomes 6–7% inflation for two or three years. The professor, on record: "Where you can add to what I know is whether there is wage pressure building up. If not — forget it, we will be fine. If you see it building, this is not temporary." The full session notes have the argument; this page is the sensor.
