Past tense. This is what the system created over the twelve months the RBI's return covers — already done, already in someone's account. Nothing is being printed as you read this.

How to read this: these are the RBI's own printed figures as of {{ site.data['money-printer'].as_of }} — stocks on a stated date, not a live feed. Money does not arrive at a per-second rate: it arrives in lumps — a CRR tranche on one date, an OMO auction on another, loans booked by banks and reported only every fortnight. Any "per second" figure here is a year's total divided by time: useful for grasping scale, useless as a description of the mechanism. The session notes walk through every number.
