## 1. What it is now — one screen
A **closed, sign-in-only study room** built on Prof. Prasanna Tantri's macro sessions.
Notes, weekly lecture recordings, the papers/circulars he shares, a UPSC question bank,
a daily discussion, flashcards, a glossary, a macro-data watch, and a listen room —
**all behind a Google login**.

The business model in one line: the **videos are the honeypot** — students sign in to watch
the weekly lecture; signing in puts them on the **Mailchimp** newsletter; every other
feature sits behind the same login. One new lecture a week, because you forget.

---

## 2. How it got here — the pivot story
It started as an **open, contribute-it-yourself** archive (edit-on-GitHub, PRs, CC-BY,
student moderators). Two problems killed that model:
1. Only 2–3 people ever contributed — the open flow added risk, not hands.
2. Gated-worthy material (his papers, RBI circulars, draft notes, recordings) was sitting
   in public. It's calibrated for the INFS cohort; without that context it misleads.

So Harsh pivoted to **closed**. The login is framed as a **gate, not a fee**. Then two big
pieces of work followed:
- **Voice.** Harsh flagged the copy read "very AI." We researched Tantri's real voice from
  4 lecture transcripts, 2,100+ of his WhatsApp messages, and his Mint columns, and
  rewrote the site in his register (see §4). He signed off: *"this version lands."*
- **Gating decision.** Harsh chose **full backend gating** (content not shipped in static
  HTML) and **Mailchimp** for the newsletter (see §6).

## 3. Access model & architecture (summary)
Full detail in `ARCHITECTURE.md`. The short version:

- **Public shell** (stays static on Render): landing/home, About, sign-in, newsletter capture. No gated content in the HTML.
- **Gated app** (auth required): after Google sign-in, content is read from **Firestore** (rules: authenticated reads only).
- **Honeypot assets, hard-gated:** video via **Cloudflare Stream signed playback tokens** (no-download); papers/audiobooks via **signed, expiring URLs**.
- **Serverless layer** (Cloudflare Worker / Firebase Functions) mints tokens, signs URLs, and calls Mailchimp with the key held **server-side**. No VPS.
- **Honest caveat:** text notes still live as Jekyll markdown → they're only truly gated once migrated to Firestore (Phase 3). Until then the wall is "soft" for text, "hard" for video/papers. This trade-off was stated to Harsh and accepted.

## 4. The voice (the thing to get right)
Reference: `VOICE-TANTRI.md` (guide) and `VOICE-RAW-TANTRI.md` (verbatim quote bank).

- Precise, blunt, urgent. Reader = junior colleague, not pupil.

## 6. Decisions log
- **Closed platform** over open-source. Rationale: low contribution + gated material exposed. (Harsh)
- **Full backend gating** over soft client-side wall or hybrid. Harsh explicitly chose the strongest option; accepts the bigger build. Content must leave static HTML (Phase 3).
- **Mailchimp** as the newsletter tool (over Buttondown/ConvertKit). Start with the embedded form (no key in browser); auto-subscribe via a function later.
- **Voice = Tantri, grounded in real material.** Generic/AI-smooth copy is rejected. Lean into his jokes/gripes/caveats.
- **Fonts** (earlier): Fraunces + Hanken Grotesk + JetBrains Mono. Navy `#20307F` + gold `#E3A310`.
- **Video host:** Cloudflare Stream (no-download/signed playback). Files could also sit on Harsh's VPS behind signed URLs.
- **Firebase web config is public by design;** the exposed apiKey is not a secret. It was restricted; do NOT rotate or scrub git history for it.
